A budget in Budget App sets a spending limit for one category over a period of time, such as \$400 for groceries each month. Create one budget for each category you want to track.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Sign in and go to the **Dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new budget">
    Select **New budget**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    - **Name**: a short label, for example "Groceries".
    - **Amount**: the most you plan to spend.
    - **Period**: weekly, monthly, or yearly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

Your new budget appears on the dashboard with its full amount available.
