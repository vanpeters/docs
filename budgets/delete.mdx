---

Delete a budget in Budget App when you stop tracking a category, such as a one-time trip budget after you get home. Deleting a budget also deletes every transaction inside it.

<Warning>
  Deleting a budget is permanent and cannot be undone. All transactions in the budget are deleted too.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the budget">
    On the **Dashboard**, select the budget you want to remove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Delete">
    Select **Edit**, then select **Delete budget** at the bottom of the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the confirmation dialog, select **Delete** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep the budget.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The budget and its transactions disappear from the dashboard.

<Tip>
  To keep your history, [edit the budget](/budgets/edit) instead of deleting it.
</Tip>
