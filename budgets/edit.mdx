Edit a budget in Budget App when you need to raise a limit, rename a category, or switch from monthly to weekly tracking. Your existing transactions stay attached to the budget.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the budget">
    On the **Dashboard**, select the budget you want to change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Edit">
    Select **Edit** in the top right of the budget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the details">
    Change the **Name**, **Amount**, or **Period**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The budget card shows the updated details, and the remaining balance is recalculated from the new amount.
