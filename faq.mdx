These are the questions Budget App users ask most often. If you don't find your answer here, contact support at `[support email]`.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I restore a deleted budget or transaction?">
    No. Deleting a budget or transaction is permanent. If you only want to hide a budget, consider editing its amount or name instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I go over budget?">
    The budget card shows a negative remaining balance and is highlighted so you can spot it. You can keep adding transactions, or [edit the budget](/budgets/edit) to raise its limit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does deleting a budget delete its transactions?">
    Yes. When you delete a budget, all transactions inside it are deleted too. See [Delete a budget](/budgets/delete).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change a budget's period?">
    Yes. Open the budget, select **Edit**, and choose a new period. Existing transactions stay in the budget.
  </Accordion>
