Budget App helps you decide where your money goes before you spend it. Create budgets for categories like groceries or rent, log transactions as they happen, and see how much you have left at a glance. This guide shows you how to manage budgets and transactions, including how to edit and delete them.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a budget">
    Pick a category, set an amount, and choose a period such as monthly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log transactions">
    Add each purchase or payment to the matching budget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track your progress">
    Watch your remaining balance update as you spend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust as you go">
    Edit or delete budgets and transactions whenever your plans change.
  </Step>
</Steps>
