This quickstart takes you from a new Budget App account to a working budget with your first transaction recorded. It should take about five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to `[your app URL]` and select **Sign up**. Enter your email and a password, then confirm your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first budget">
    On the dashboard, select **New budget**. Enter a name such as "Groceries", an amount, and a period, then select **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a transaction">
    Open the budget and select **Add transaction**. Enter the amount, date, and a short note, then select **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your balance">
    The budget card now shows how much you have spent and how much remains for the period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Create a few budgets for your biggest spending categories first. You can add more later.
</Tip>
