A transaction in Budget App records a single purchase or payment. Adding transactions as you spend keeps each budget's remaining balance accurate.

## Prerequisites

You need at least one budget. See [Create a budget](/budgets/create).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the budget">
    On the **Dashboard**, select the budget the purchase belongs to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add transaction">
    Select **Add transaction**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    - **Amount**: how much you spent.
    - **Date**: when it happened.
    - **Note**: optional, for example "Weekly shop".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The transaction appears in the budget's list and the remaining balance goes down by the amount you entered.
